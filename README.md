## 📌 Executive Summary
This project evaluates transactional retail data across 9,994 orders to uncover customer lifetime patterns, churn risks, and revenue concentration. Using Python in Google Colab, the analysis implements **RFM Segmentation**, **Monthly Cohort Retention Heatmaps**, and **Pareto (80/20 Rule) Analysis**.

## 📈 Visualizations & Key Findings

### 1. RFM Customer Segmentation
![RFM Segments](./rfm_customer_segments_distribution.png)
- **Champions & Loyalists:** Comprise over 63% of the customer base and generate ~84% of total revenue.
- **At-Risk Opportunity:** 178 customers represent ~$262,000 in past revenue with an average inactivity period exceeding 177 days.

### 2. Cohort Retention Heatmap
![Cohort Heatmap](./monthly_cohort_retention_heatmap.png)
- A significant drop-off is observed immediately after Month 1 across all cohorts, highlighting the need for early re-engagement workflows.

### 3. Pareto Revenue Concentration
![Pareto Curve](./pareto_revenue_concentration_curve.png)
- Top 20% customers (158 users) drive ~48% of enterprise revenue, while top 50% drive ~80%.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. **VIP Loyalty Tier:** Prioritize the top 20% spenders with dedicated support and exclusive rewards to prevent churn.
2. **Win-Back Campaigns:** Target the 178 "At Risk" customers using automated personalized email incentives within 60 days of inactivity.
3. **Onboarding Follow-Up:** Introduce a 30-day post-first-purchase discount sequence to improve cohort retention stability.
